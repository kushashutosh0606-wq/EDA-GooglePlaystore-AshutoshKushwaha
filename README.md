Overview
EDA-Projects is a collection of exploratory data analysis (EDA) projects designed to extract valuable insights from diverse datasets. Each project demonstrates a structured approach to data analysis, featuring techniques such as data cleaning, visualization, and pattern recognition.

This repository serves as a practical guide for beginners, offering examples that illustrate how to effectively explore and interpret data. Whether you’re interested in understanding trends, making data-driven decisions, or simply experimenting with new datasets, EDA-Projects provides a comprehensive resource to enhance your analytical skills.
📱 Google Playstore Data Project
About the Dataset
Source: Downloaded from Kaggle.

Context: While Apple App Store datasets are widely available, Google Play Store data is harder to obtain due to dynamic page loading with JQuery. This dataset was scraped directly from the Play Store, making it a rare and valuable resource.

Content: Each app entry includes attributes such as:
Category Rating Size Installs, reviews, and more
Acknowledgements
This dataset was scraped from the Google Play Store, without which this analysis would not be possible.

Inspiration
The Play Store apps data has enormous potential to drive app-making businesses to success. Actionable insights can be drawn for developers to:

Identify trends in app categories and ratings

Discover factors influencing app success

Capture opportunities in the Android market

✨ With this repository, you’ll gain hands-on experience in EDA techniques while uncovering meaningful insights from one of the most dynamic datasets available.
